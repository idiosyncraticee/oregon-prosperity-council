Governor’s Prosperity Council
The Prosperity Council is an outside advisory group convened by Governor Kotek to help
shape strategies for Oregon’s economic prosperity and growth. The council includes
representatives from different regions of the state, key industries, and labor, and is focused on
three main areas: business climate, workforce, and tools for growth. Its role is to develop
practical recommendations for the near, medium, and long term in each of these areas.
Council meetings began in January 2026 and will conclude in June 2026, when a final report
of recommendations will be delivered. This statewide survey will be circulated widely and will
remain open through March 20, 2026 so the council can consider this input as it develops its
recommendations. As a note, all survey responses will be included in the public record. The
survey will take approximately 15 minutes to complete.
