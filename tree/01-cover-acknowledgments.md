## TL;DR  *(generated · confidence: high)*

Cover page and acknowledgments for the Oregon Prosperity Council's June 2026 report to Governor Tina Kotek titled 'Recommendations for Oregon's Long-Term Competitiveness & Prosperity.' The report is co-led by Renée James (Ampere Computing) and Curtis Robinhold (Port of Portland), with a 13-member council representing Oregon business, labor, and nonprofit sectors. It acknowledges support from ECOnorthwest and Gard Communications, and input from Oregonians throughout the engagement process.
