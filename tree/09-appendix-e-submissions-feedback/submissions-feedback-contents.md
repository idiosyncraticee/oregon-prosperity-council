# Submissions & Feedback — Contents

<!-- enrich:begin -->

## TL;DR  *(generated · confidence: high)*

This page lists the 47 organizations, companies, individuals, and agencies that submitted feedback to the Oregon Prosperity Council during its consultation process. Submitters span the private sector (Microsoft, Pacific Power), agriculture and food production (Tillamook County Creamery, Oregon Farm Bureau), labor representatives (SEIU), universities, local government, and advocacy groups, arranged chronologically by submission date.

**Key points** *(each cites a PDF page)*:

- Total of 47 submitted documents in Appendix E ([p. 93](https://www.oregon.gov/gov/Documents/Oregon%20Prosperity%20Council%20Report_June%202026.pdf#page=93))
- Documents arranged chronologically by date of submittal ([p. 93](https://www.oregon.gov/gov/Documents/Oregon%20Prosperity%20Council%20Report_June%202026.pdf#page=93))
- Private sector participation from Microsoft Corporation and Pacific Power ([p. 93](https://www.oregon.gov/gov/Documents/Oregon%20Prosperity%20Council%20Report_June%202026.pdf#page=93))
- University sector well-represented: University of Oregon, Oregon State University, Oregon Tech, Oregon Public Universities ([p. 93](https://www.oregon.gov/gov/Documents/Oregon%20Prosperity%20Council%20Report_June%202026.pdf#page=93))
- Repeat submissions from same organizations: Portland Metro Chamber (3 times), Energy Trust of Oregon (2 times), Oregon State University (2 times), Oregon Business & Industry (2 times) ([p. 93](https://www.oregon.gov/gov/Documents/Oregon%20Prosperity%20Council%20Report_June%202026.pdf#page=93))
